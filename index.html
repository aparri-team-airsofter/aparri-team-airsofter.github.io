MEMBERSHIP INFORMATION

WHO CAN JOIN?

People interested in responsible airsoft, teamwork and respectful sportsmanship. Minors should participate only with parent or guardian permission and in accordance with applicable rules.

MEMBER EXPECTATIONS

  • Follow field rules and safety briefings.
  • Wear appropriate eye and face protection.
  • Respect teammates, opponents and venue staff.
  • Use airsoft equipment responsibly and lawfully.
  • Confirm membership requirements with team organizers.

HOW TO JOIN

Send a registration request or message the team through its official Facebook page. Organizers will provide current membership details, fees (if any), and next steps.
